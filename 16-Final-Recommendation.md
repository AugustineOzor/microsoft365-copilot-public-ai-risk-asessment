# 16 — Final Governance Recommendation

## Decision

### APPROVE WITH CONTROLS

Microsoft 365 Copilot may be used for approved workplace productivity and knowledge-management activities provided the required governance controls are implemented.

## Approval Conditions

1. Copilot is listed in the AI system inventory.
2. Approved and prohibited uses are documented.
3. High-risk use cases receive governance review.
4. Sensitive-data rules are communicated to users.
5. Public-facing content receives human approval.
6. Collection, metadata, and cultural heritage outputs receive expert review.
7. Meeting summaries are reviewed before becoming official records.
8. Users complete Responsible AI training.
9. Permissions and access controls are reviewed for high-risk areas.
10. Incidents are reported and tracked.
11. Governance metrics are monitored through a dashboard.
12. The assessment is periodically reviewed as Copilot capabilities and organizational use evolve.

## Residual Risk

**Medium**, provided the controls remain active and are periodically tested.

## Conclusion

Copilot should be treated as a powerful AI assistant rather than an authority for official records, public content, sensitive decisions, or cultural interpretation.

The organization should continuously reassess risk as AI capabilities, use cases, data environments, and governance requirements evolve.
