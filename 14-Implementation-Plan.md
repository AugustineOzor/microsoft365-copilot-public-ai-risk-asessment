# 14 — Implementation Plan

## Phase 1 — Discovery
Identify departmental Copilot use, use cases, data types, and public outputs.

## Phase 2 — Risk Assessment
Build the risk register, classify use cases, identify restrictions, and assign owners.

## Phase 3 — Control Design
Define the human-review matrix, public-content workflow, meeting-summary review, and data-handling rules.

## Phase 4 — Policy and Training
Publish acceptable-use guidance, train staff, and provide prompt-safety guidance.

## Phase 5 — Monitoring
Maintain the governance dashboard, track incidents and high-risk use, and review audit evidence.

## Phase 6 — Continuous Improvement
Update controls, review permissions, refresh training and policies, and periodically reassess risk.

```text
DISCOVERY → RISK ASSESSMENT → CONTROL DESIGN → POLICY & TRAINING → MONITORING → CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
```
