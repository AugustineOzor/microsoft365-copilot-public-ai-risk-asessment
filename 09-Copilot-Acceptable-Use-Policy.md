# 09 — Copilot Acceptable Use Policy

## Approved Uses
- Draft internal content
- Summarize non-sensitive documents
- Brainstorm report structures
- Draft meeting summaries
- Draft emails
- Improve readability
- Create presentation outlines
- Find information the user is authorized to access

## Conditional Uses
Require additional review for object descriptions, metadata, exhibition text, public communications, visitor FAQs, meeting minutes, policy summaries, and translation drafts.

## Prohibited or Restricted Uses
Copilot must not be the final authority for object authentication, valuation, legal interpretation, HR decisions, performance evaluation, donor eligibility, sensitive acquisitions, repatriation, restricted cultural interpretation, or confidential records processing without approval.

> Copilot assists work; authorized humans remain accountable for official decisions and records.
