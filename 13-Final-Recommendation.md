# 13 · Final Governance Recommendation

## Decision

### ✅ Approve with Controls

Microsoft 365 Copilot may be used for approved workplace productivity and knowledge-management tasks, provided that governance controls are implemented.

## Approval Conditions

1. Copilot must be listed in the AI system inventory.
2. Approved and prohibited uses must be documented.
3. High-risk use cases must receive governance review.
4. Sensitive data rules must be communicated to users.
5. Public-facing content must receive human approval.
6. Collection, metadata, and cultural heritage outputs must receive expert review.
7. Meeting summaries must be reviewed before becoming official records.
8. Users must complete responsible AI training.
9. Permissions and access controls must be reviewed for high-risk areas.
10. Incidents must be reported and tracked.
11. Copilot governance metrics must be monitored through a dashboard.
12. The assessment must be reviewed periodically as Copilot capabilities and organizational use evolve.

## Implementation Roadmap

**Phase 1 — Discovery**
- Identify how departments use Microsoft 365 Copilot
- Map use cases by department
- Identify data types and public-facing outputs

**Phase 2 — Risk Assessment**
- Build risk register
- Classify use cases by risk tier
- Identify restricted uses

**Phase 3 — Control Design**
- Define human review matrix
- Define public content approval workflow
- Define meeting-summary review process
- Define data handling rules

**Phase 4 — Policy and Training**
- Draft Copilot acceptable use guidance
- Create training for staff
- Create prompt safety guidance

**Phase 5 — Monitoring**
- Build Copilot governance dashboard
- Track incidents, training, and high-risk use cases
- Review audit evidence

**Phase 6 — Continuous Improvement**
- Update controls based on incidents
- Review permissions
- Update policy and training
- Reassess risk periodically
