# 01 — Project Charter

## Project Name
**Microsoft 365 Copilot Public AI Risk Assessment**

## Purpose
Create a practical governance plan for safe, responsible, and auditable use of Microsoft 365 Copilot in a museum, records-management, and metadata environment.

## Governance
- **Business Sponsor:** Digital Transformation / Operations Leadership
- **Governance Owner:** AI Governance Committee
- **System Owner:** IT / Microsoft 365 Administration

## Business Owners
Registration, Collections, Records Management, Exhibitions, Communications, Visitor Services, Education, and Administration.

## Risk Owners
Data Governance, Privacy, Records Management, Information Security, Legal / Compliance, and Department Heads.

## Objectives
Produce an AI system profile, use-case inventory, risk register, NIST AI RMF-aligned assessment, governance controls, acceptable-use rules, human-review workflows, monitoring dashboard, incident response plan, audit checklist, and final recommendation.

## Scope
Covers internal drafting, meeting/document summarization, Microsoft 365 search, metadata drafting, public-content drafting before review, email/chat summarization, and related Word, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams workflows.

Out of scope: legal advice, penetration testing, tenant configuration, licensing recommendations, actual deployment, private configuration claims, and employee performance evaluation.
