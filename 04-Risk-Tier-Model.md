# 04 · Risk Tier Model for Copilot Use

| Risk Tier | Description | Example | Governance Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Low** | Personal productivity with non-sensitive content | Formatting a draft note | Basic responsible AI guidance |
| **Medium** | Internal business content or records impact | Meeting summary, policy summary | Human review and retention awareness |
| **High** | Public-facing, cultural, collection, or official-record impact | Exhibition text, object description | Department approval and expert review |
| **Restricted** | Confidential, legal, HR, donor, sensitive cultural, or acquisition content | Legal memo, donor record, restricted provenance summary | Prohibited unless formally approved |
