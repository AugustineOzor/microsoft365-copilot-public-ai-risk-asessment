# 04 — Risk-Tier Model

| Tier | Description | Example | Requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | Non-sensitive productivity | Formatting a draft | Basic Responsible AI guidance |
| Medium | Internal business or records impact | Meeting summary | Human review and retention awareness |
| High | Public, cultural, collection, or official-record impact | Exhibition text | Department approval and expert review |
| Restricted | Confidential, legal, HR, donor, sensitive cultural, or acquisition content | Restricted provenance summary | Prohibited unless formally approved |

Risk increases with sensitive data, official records, public content, cultural heritage, legal/compliance decisions, high-impact decisions, or restricted information.
