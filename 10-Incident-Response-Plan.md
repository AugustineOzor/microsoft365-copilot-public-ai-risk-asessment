# 10 · Copilot Incident Response Plan

## Level 1: Minor Output Issue

**Example:** Copilot produces unclear wording or a minor formatting error.

**Response:**
- User corrects output
- No escalation unless repeated

## Level 2: Inaccurate Internal Output

**Example:** Copilot summarizes a meeting incorrectly.

**Response:**
- Correct the summary
- Notify meeting owner
- Record issue if business-impacting
- Update review checklist

## Level 3: Sensitive or Records-Impacting Issue

**Example:** Copilot output includes confidential information in a draft that is shared too broadly.

**Response:**
- Restrict access
- Notify data or records owner
- Review permissions
- Correct or remove content
- Document root cause

## Level 4: Public, Legal, Privacy, or Reputational Incident

**Example:** AI-assisted public content contains a serious factual, cultural, or confidentiality issue.

**Response:**
- Remove or correct content
- Notify governance owner
- Escalate to leadership
- Preserve evidence
- Conduct root-cause review
- Update controls and training
