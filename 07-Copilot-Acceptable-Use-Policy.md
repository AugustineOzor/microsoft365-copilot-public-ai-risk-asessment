# 07 · Governance Controls & Acceptable Use Policy

## Governance Control Framework

Ten control categories cover the Copilot governance program end to end.

### 1. Human Oversight Controls (Most Critical)

**Purpose:** Ensure Copilot remains a support tool and not a decision-maker.

**Controls:**
- Human review of all high-risk outputs
- Meeting owner validation of summaries
- Registrar review of metadata-related outputs
- Curatorial review of collection and cultural content
- Communications review of public-facing content
- No automatic publication

**Why it matters:** Copilot can produce fluent content that appears correct while containing factual errors or unsupported conclusions.

### 2. Data Protection and Access Controls

**Purpose:** Prevent exposure of sensitive information and reduce oversharing risks.

**Controls:**
- Data-classification guidance
- Permissions review before rollout
- Sensitivity labels
- Retention policies
- Restricted-use categories
- User training on confidential information

Microsoft states that Copilot respects identity models and permissions, inherits sensitivity labels, applies retention policies, and supports audit capabilities depending on configuration.¹

**Protected information examples:** Donor information · HR records · Legal documents · Acquisition negotiations · Restricted provenance records · Sensitive cultural heritage information

### 3. Content Accuracy Controls

**Purpose:** Reduce hallucinations and inaccurate outputs.

**Controls:**
- Source verification
- Fact-checking requirements
- Human approval before official use
- Draft-content designation
- Review checklists

**Where applied:** Object descriptions · Metadata drafts · Meeting summaries · Policy summaries · Public communications · Exhibition content

### 4. Public Content Governance Controls

**Purpose:** Prevent inaccurate AI-generated content from reaching visitors or the public.

**Controls:**
- Communications-team review
- Curatorial review for cultural content
- Publication approval workflow
- Escalation process for sensitive topics
- AI-generated content treated as draft material

**Approval workflow:** Copilot Draft → Expert Review → Content Approval → Publication

### 5. Records and Documentation Controls

**Purpose:** Protect the integrity of official business records.

**Controls:**
- Meeting-summary review
- Official records created by humans
- Retention policy application
- Records classification
- Documentation of approvals

**Example:** AI-generated meeting summaries remain draft records until reviewed and approved by the meeting owner.

### 6. Cultural Heritage Protection Controls

**Purpose:** Prevent inaccurate or insensitive interpretation of museum collections.

**Controls:**
- Curatorial validation
- Cultural sensitivity review
- Restricted use for sacred or sensitive subjects
- Approved terminology guidance
- Human approval before publication

**Examples:** Sacred artifacts · Indigenous collections · Religious collections · Contested historical narratives · Human remains documentation

### 7. Responsible AI User Controls

**Purpose:** Reduce misuse and over-reliance on AI.

**Controls:**
- Responsible AI training
- Prompt guidance
- Hallucination awareness
- Human accountability requirements
- User education on AI limitations

**Key governance principle:** AI output = draft content. Human review = required judgment.

### 8. Security and Prompt-Injection Controls

**Purpose:** Reduce information-security risks.

**Controls:**
- Security monitoring
- User awareness training
- Reporting suspicious outputs
- Microsoft security protections
- Incident management process

Microsoft states that Microsoft 365 Copilot includes protections such as blocking harmful content, detecting protected material, and addressing prompt-injection attacks.²

### 9. Monitoring and Audit Controls

**Purpose:** Track governance effectiveness and identify issues early.

**Controls:**
- Governance dashboard
- Incident tracking
- Training-completion monitoring
- Public-content review monitoring
- Audit-log review
- High-risk use-case reviews

**Monitored metrics:**

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Training Completion | >95% |
| Public Content Review | 100% |
| Meeting Summary Review | 100% |
| Restricted Data Incidents | 0 |
| Critical Incidents | 0 |
| High-Risk Use Cases Reviewed | 100% |

### 10. Incident Response Controls

**Purpose:** Manage Copilot-related governance failures.

**Controls:**
- Incident severity classification
- Escalation procedures
- Content correction process
- Root-cause analysis
- User retraining where required

**Severity examples:**

| Severity | Example |
|---|---|
| Level 1 | Minor formatting issue |
| Level 2 | Incorrect meeting summary |
| Level 3 | Sensitive content exposure |
| Level 4 | Public misinformation or major privacy incident |

## Acceptable Use Policy

### Approved Uses

Staff may use Copilot for:
- Drafting internal content
- Summarizing non-sensitive documents
- Brainstorming structure for reports
- Creating first-draft meeting summaries
- Drafting emails
- Improving readability
- Creating presentation outlines
- Finding information the user is authorized to access

### Conditional Uses

Copilot may be used with extra review for:
- Object descriptions
- Metadata wording
- Exhibition text
- Public communications
- Visitor-facing FAQs
- Meeting minutes
- Policy summaries
- Translation drafts

### Prohibited or Restricted Uses

Copilot must not be used as the final authority for:
- Object authentication
- Object valuation
- Legal interpretation
- HR decisions
- Staff performance evaluation
- Donor eligibility decisions
- Sensitive acquisition decisions
- Repatriation decisions
- Restricted cultural interpretation
- Confidential records processing without approval
