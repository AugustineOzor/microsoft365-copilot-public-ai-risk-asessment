# 05 · NIST AI RMF Alignment

The NIST Generative AI Profile is a companion resource to the AI Risk Management Framework for generative AI, intended to help organizations incorporate trustworthiness considerations into the design, development, use, and evaluation of generative AI systems.¹

This assessment is organized under the NIST-inspired Govern–Map–Measure–Manage structure:

| Function | Application in This Assessment |
|---|---|
| **Govern** | Define ownership, policy, and accountability |
| **Map** | Identify use cases, data, users, and context |
| **Measure** | Assess risks, outputs, quality, and incidents |
| **Manage** | Apply controls, monitoring, training, and remediation |

The NIST Generative AI Profile identifies risks that are unique to or exacerbated by generative AI, including confabulation, data privacy, harmful bias or homogenization, human-AI configuration risks such as overreliance and automation bias, information integrity, information security, prompt injection, and intellectual property.¹ ²
